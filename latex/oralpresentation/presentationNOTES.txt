[Synchronization and Resource Management]
    MOST IMPORTANT part, how my Architecture works?
    // Read

"Dual semaphores are designed for a bounded Producer-Consumer queue with a 
pre-allocated pool of worker threads constantly waiting to pop work.
 Because my architecture spawns client handler threads dynamically with pthread_create(),
  there is no consumer queue. Using a Mutex and Condition Variable (roomAvailable)
allows the main thread to safely inspect the buffer, throttle when
 capacity reaches MAX_THREADS, and cleanly track client state."
